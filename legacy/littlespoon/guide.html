Quick Start Guide
Pediatric Nutrition Tools
Open in any browser: pat-dubois.github.io/peds-nutrition-calc/index-v2.html

Little Spoon is a pediatric nutrition calculator with three tools in one page. It works on your phone, tablet, or computer. Bookmark it for quick access between patients.

Privacy: No patient data is ever saved or sent anywhere. When you close the page, everything is gone. It also works offline once loaded.

All three tabs share the Patient section at the top (weight, height, gender). Fill that in first, then pick your tab. The Reset button clears everything across all three tabs at once.

Tab 1 Nutrition

Calculates daily energy, protein, and fluid requirements.

What to enter
  • Weight (kg) and Height (cm) in the Patient section
  • Gender - tap Male or Female
  • Age in years and months
  • Activity level - only needed for children 3 years and older

Tap Calculate and you'll get three results:

Energy
1,245 kcal/day
Protein
23.8 g/day
Fluid
1,500 mL/day

Each result shows the per-kg rate in brackets. Tap Show calculation under any result to see the full formula and step-by-step math.

Tab 2 Growth Charts

WHO growth chart Z-scores and percentiles for children 0 to 5 years.

What to enter
  • Weight, Height, and Gender in the Patient section
  • Date of Birth and Date of Measurement (defaults to today)
  • Head circumference - optional, only shows results if entered
  • BMI or Weight-for-Length - toggle between the two

Tap Calculate to get Z-scores and percentiles for:

  • Weight-for-Age
  • Length-for-Age (under 2) or Height-for-Age (2 and up)
  • BMI-for-Age or Weight-for-Length (your choice)
  • Head Circumference-for-Age (when entered)

Z-Score Color Coding

Green - Normal range
Gold - Mild concern (Z ±1 to ±2)
Orange - Moderate (Z ±2 to ±3)
Red - Severe (Z beyond ±3)
Note: The Growth tab covers ages 0-5 years (0-60 months), which is the range of the WHO Child Growth Standards.

Tab 3 DRI Reference

Quick-reference table of daily vitamin and mineral requirements by age group. Covers 14 vitamins and 12 minerals across all pediatric age ranges.

What to enter
  • Gender in the Patient section
  • Age group from the dropdown (0-6 months through 14-18 years)
Important: For the 9-13 years and 14-18 years age groups, you must select Male or Female first. Some nutrient needs differ by gender at those ages. The table won't load without a gender selected.

Reading the Table

Teal values = RDA (Recommended Dietary Allowance) - backed by strong research evidence. Set to meet the needs of 97-98% of healthy children in that age group.
Blue values with asterisk (*) = AI (Adequate Intake) - best available estimate when research isn't conclusive enough to set an RDA. Still the official recommendation.
UL UL column = Tolerable Upper Intake Level - the maximum daily amount unlikely to cause harm. A dash means no upper limit has been established.
